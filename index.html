Personal Interests

I'm a huge fan of Cars. They are extremely interesting and have so much engineering into them, it's really fascinating. Cars have a lot in common when it
comes to developing. When there is an issue with a car, we need to begin diagnosing what's going on, just like you'd do with bugs. You find the common
problems and then go from there. A car is literally a computer with 4 wheels. Whenever I'm having a bad day, or even a great day, going on a drive and
listening to music is literally what keeps me alive. I probably go on a drive every day because it's a very free feeling. I can just not exist in my mind.

Another thing I'm really interested in is Psychology and overall Mental Health. I suffer with depression, anxiety, adhd, and a few other things. At first,
I didn't really care to be all that interested in them, but they impact my life significantly. Being able to look "deeper" into myself and try to begin helping
these illnesses has really helped me in many ways. I've spent a lot of time taking courses, going to therapy and other things to really get a grasp on how
to cope with myself and these differences. These things have taught me extremely valuable coping skills and other things. One thing I really enjoy is being
able to spread awareness to those around me. To help people find help and give them the resources that they need. I still have a very long way to go, but I'm
improving every day and that's what matters most. The hardest part of doing anything is starting.

Lastly, I really enjoy Photography. I don't really have a specific type of photos that I like to take, but I do tend to take a lot of car and nature photos. However,
I don't mind taking photos of my friends and other things. Actually, I've been trying to take more photos of things that I determine are good memories. I want
to have these photos to look back on and remember and cherish those moments. I wouldn't say I'm that great or anything at photography, but it is something that
really helps me decompress, and disassociate. It allows me to be thankful and enjoy what's around me and truly see the beauty in everything. You can take a
photo of literally anything and make it beautiful. That's what I love. It's helped me appreciate the little things that people tend to not really care about.
